Tax return fields used in grattan assessments
=============================================

Scope and sources
-----------------
The ATO individuals' sample indexes checked here cover all 13 income years
from 2011-12 to 2023-24. Earlier public files were 1% samples. The aLife
variable manual snapshot (27 March 2026) covers 1990-91 to 2022-23. A catalogue entry
identifies a variable available for selection in that year. An extract can
omit it or contain missing values. Names below are column names, without the
aLife catalogue's itr_ or mcs_ table prefix. The taxstats 0.1.0.1415
files for 2011-12 through 2013-14 use the alias age_range. Supply one row per person and
year, with spouse and super data aligned to that same person and year.

Senior eligibility, family status and Medicare
---------------------------------------------
2% sample. Age_range gives five-year age groups, with open-ended youngest
and oldest groups. Gender is 0 for male and 1 for female. Partner_status
records whether a spouse date of birth was reported. The indexes contain
no senior claim code, exact age, spouse age or dependant count.
Spouse_adjusted_taxable_inc occurs in 2011-12 through 2013-14 only. It is
a proxy for spouse taxable and rebate income. Later indexes omit even this
amount. A single-year sample cannot link a spouse's return reliably.

aLife. c_age_30_june is age in completed years. c_gender uses the opposite
coding (0 female, 1 male). c_pension_age_eligible and sp_pension_age_eligible
can resolve age eligibility where a half-year threshold falls within an age
in completed years. on_sato_cd identifies the historical category through
2011-12 and on_sapto_cd identifies the category from 2012-13. These codes
distinguish illness separation and spouse eligibility. sp_flag and
c_depend_child describe family circumstances. sp_taxable_income_loss supplies
the spouse income for Medicare and the basic-tax test for unused senior
offsets. spc_rebate_income supplies the senior combined-income test from
2009-10. Through 2008-09 the senior test uses taxable income. Standalone
sapto() accepts taxable_income and spouse_taxable_income separately.
From 2012-13 the transfer test includes the exempt pensions specified in
reg 12(5). sp_pension_exempt supplies the spouse's amount. A caller can
supply sapto_exempt_pension for the individual. The broader it_gov_pension
can include other payments. Neither addback changes Medicare taxable income.

Fallback. Without a claim category, income_tax() infers it from age and
family information. A partnered taxpayer is assumed to live with an eligible
spouse unless supplied evidence indicates otherwise. Age bands use the
package's representative ages. A caller can provide fractional age where
the return supplies no age, or add c_age_30_june alongside a sample band.
Missing sex uses the male pension age. Missing age defaults to 42, missing dependants
to zero, and missing spouse income to the other available spouse measure or
zero. Spouse_taxable_income is a caller alias, absent from the checked 2%
indexes. Either source may lack the facts for pension residence, an eligible
under-age pension, illness separation, part-year family circumstances, or
trustee-assessed income. The supplementary veteran codes V/W/X identify
which spouse was a veteran and do not establish all those conditions.
Standalone medicare_levy() accepts the taxpayer's senior status. It uses the
ordinary spouse threshold unless spouse entitlement can be derived in
income_tax(). Its historical pto flag does not calculate the pensioner rebate.

Rebate income
-------------
2% sample. Net_rent_amt is signed. Net_fincl_invstmt_lss_amt,
Rptbl_Empr_spr_cont_amt and Non_emp_spr_amt identify financial losses,
reportable employer super and deductible personal super. Rep_frng_ben_amt
combines the employer classes. The field can reproduce the 2016-17 worksheet,
which adjusts both classes together. From 2017-18 the calculation needs the
split. With only a total the package treats it as exempt-employer benefits.
The indexes omit the assessable first home super saver (FHSS) release.

aLife. ic_rebate_income and spc_rebate_income supply ATO-calculated rebate
income from 2009-10 and take priority. For reconstruction, it_property_loss
is a positive rental loss and is_net_rent is signed. it_invest_loss,
it_rept_empl_super_cont and ds_pers_super_cont are the other addbacks.
it_rept_fringe_benefit_exe and it_rept_fringe_benefit_nexe are separate
from 2016-17. From 2017-18 only the exempt-employer amount is discounted.
From 2018-19 is_other_cat3_fhss is the assessable release. tws_assessable_fhss
records withholding. Spouse components include sp_rept_super_cont,
sp_net_invest_loss (already includes rental loss), sp_rept_fringe_benefit
and its _exe/_nexe split. A caller can supply spouse_assessable_fhss. Without
the calculated spouse total either source may lack a spouse adjustment.
Missing components contribute zero.

Small business income tax offset
--------------------------------
2% sample. Net_PP_BI_amt and Net_NPP_BI_amt are net sole-trader amounts.
Total_PP_BI_amt and Total_NPP_BI_amt approximate turnover, and the comparable
BE columns supply expenses if net fields are absent. Net_PT_PP_dsn and
Net_PT_NPP_dsn include general distributions and cannot identify eligible
partnership or trust business income. The sample-based estimate therefore
omits qualifying shares and can misclassify a sole trader.

aLife. From 2015-16 isn_sbi_net (label 15A) and pt_share_sbi_pships and
pt_share_sbi_trusts (labels 13D and 13E) identify the return's eligible
amounts. The entity-level turnover test is already reflected in these
labels. Reported zero is respected. Tot_net_small_business_inc is an optional
complete eligible total, rather than a field in the annual 2% indexes.

Either source can lack current or prior aggregated turnover including
connected entities and affiliates, the facts for the reasonable-estimate
test, or the adjustments making an ordinary business profit eligible.
Supply small_business_eligible as a determined status, or aggregated_turnover
and previous_aggregated_turnover for a continuing business. The eligible
partnership and trust shares are independent of the sole trader's turnover.

Working Australians tax offset, from 2027-28
------------------------------------------
Neither series reviewed contains a return from a year of operation or a
complete statutory net labour income field. For projections, supply amounts
appropriate to the model year. income_tax() takes taxable income after all
applicable deductions. It does not deduct the standard amount a second time
from that taxable income. net_labour_income is a caller-supplied complete
amount after the deductions in ITAA 1997 s61-155(2).

2% sample. Sw_amt, Alow_ben_amt and ETP_txbl_amt identify employment items.
WRE_car_amt, WRE_trvl_amt, WRE_uniform_amt, WRE_self_amt and WRE_other_amt
supply work expenses. Net_PP_BI_amt and Net_NPP_BI_amt supply net sole-trader
income. Broad partnership/trust distributions and passive income are excluded.

aLife. i_salary_wage, i_allowances, i_etp and assessable i_lsum_a/i_lsum_b
identify employment income. The five d_wre_ fields supply work expenses.
is_bus_pp/is_bus_npp give net sole-trader results. i_attributed_psi and
is_psi_net cover distinct personal services income. Upfront employee share
discounts are derived from i_ess after removing deferred and pre-2009 amounts
when the breakdown permits. Otherwise only in_ess_disc_inelgbl is included.
A known nonresident c_resident value excludes this offset.

For both sources the calculation assumes that the identified work expenses
compete with the standard deduction, and uses the greater of those expenses
and up to $1,000 of qualifying employment income. Sole-trader income alone
does not attract that deduction. Either source may omit or aggregate paid
parental leave, labour-hire receipts, ESS discounts and deduction attribution.
Use net_labour_income for those cases. With no identified labour amounts the
offset is zero. Its maximum is $250 and it cannot reduce Medicare.

Flood levy and beneficiary offset
---------------------------------
2% sample. The 2011-12 index omits the flood-exemption box. With no supplied
indicator income_tax() applies the ordinary flood levy. aLife's 2011-12
tn_flood_levy_exempt supplies the exemption. Neither postcode nor levy-year
income alone establishes disaster eligibility when the indicator is missing.

bto() accepts the amount of qualifying benefits. The 2% field
Aust_govt_pnsn_allw_amt combines return items 5 and 6. aLife separates
i_gov_payments from i_gov_pension. The combined public field alone cannot
establish the amount of rebatable benefits for this standalone calculation.

Concessional contributions and Division 293
-------------------------------------------
2% sample. The checked indexes for 2011-12 and 2012-13 omit the employer
and personal fund totals. From 2013-14 through 2017-18 the field prefixes
are MCS_ (Emplr_Contr, Prsnl_Contr, Othr_Contr, Ttl_Acnt_Bal). From 2018-19
through 2023-24 they are Spr_ with the same suffixes. Non_emp_spr_amt is the
personal contribution deduction. The sample does not identify previously
assessed excess, the concessional part of other contributions, or the
person's available carry-forward cap. A closing balance alone is insufficient.

aLife. Match ITR and super data at person/year level. sc_empl_cont,
sc_pers_cont, ds_pers_super_cont (or sc_pers_cont_deductible) and, from
2018-19, sc_other_concess_cont distinguish contribution types. use_other_contr
counts the entire other bucket as concessional, an explicit upper-bound
assumption. Apply it before calculating the excess. An observed cap field
sn_concess_cont_cap is used when no cap policy parameters are supplied.
Check whether the delivered cap covers each person's unused amounts. A
separate effective cap vector can be supplied with age_based_cap = FALSE.

For an assessment containing i_excess_super, replace that included amount
with the newly modelled excess. For a matched post-2013 reform assessment,
sc_excess_concess_cont is a fallback. Without either, the helper assumes
input taxable income before any excess inclusion. The 2% sample cannot tell
whether its taxable income already includes an excess. The income test uses
the resulting taxable income, full reportable fringes and net investment
losses. aLife adds as_family_trust_inc_txd and subtracts is_other_cat3_fhss
where present. A caller can supply zero_rate_super_lump_sum. The full
taxable lump-sum field does not identify its zero-rated portion.

Both sources may need additional information about contribution history,
defined-benefit interests, special contribution exclusions and the zero-rate
lump-sum component. The helper models accumulation contributions with the
specified cap and threshold. Its historical defaults are $30,000/$35,000
and $300,000. Specify the settings for the assessment year.
For a custom age-based cap, aLife's c_age_30_june allows the exact age
test. The 2% sample supplies only a band. For example, a cutoff of 60
includes the whole 60-64 band. See cap2_age in apply_super_caps_and_div293().
