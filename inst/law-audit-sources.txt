Tax-law corrections in grattan 2026.3.3

This source register accompanies the regression tests named test-law-*.R.
The audit baseline was afc57647cc73631507bd11a7df7c00bffb1cd997. Expected
amounts in the tests are derived from the provisions and dated worksheets
below, rather than copied from the former package output or internal tables.

Small-business offset (test-law-offsets.R / test-law-integration.R)

Rates 5%, 8%, 13%, 16% apply from 2015-16, 2016-17, 2020-21 and 2021-22
respectively. Turnover limit rises from $2m to $5m from 2016-17. The business
income fraction cannot exceed one. The offset is non-refundable, capped at
$1,000 and reduces ordinary tax before Medicare and temporary levies:
https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/income-deductions-and-concessions/income-and-deductions-for-business/concessions-offsets-and-rebates/small-business-income-tax-offset

https://www.legislation.gov.au/bills/C2018B00199EM_1.pdf

Remaining input assumptions

An explicit SAPTO claim code denotes actual entitlement, including eligible
under-age pensioners. Without it the package infers from available age and
family status; it cannot reconstruct missing pension/veteran/residency facts.
Medicare's standalone API has no spouse-entitlement argument and uses the
spouse's ordinary threshold. income_tax can use the paired claim codes.
When spouse taxable income is absent, the older adjusted-income field remains
an approximation; separate supplied taxable and rebate fields remain separate.
Aggregate modern fringe-benefit fields do not identify exempt versus other
employers; an explicitly supplied ic_rebate_income overrides reconstruction.
Historical CGT policy rescaling outside the clearly documented supported
period, asset-level reconstruction, population costing and unavailable facts
are outside these fixes.
